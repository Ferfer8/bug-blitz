Frequently Asked Questions

Log in, choose Easy or Hard mode, then hold down your mouse or trackpad and swipe across the bugs to blitz them. Each bug is worth 1 point. Blitz as many as you can before the 2-minute timer runs out.

Swiping a shoe takes 10 seconds off your timer, and the timer flashes red. Let go of the mouse to move past shoes safely.

In Hard mode, bugs and shoes move much faster and more of them appear at once. Each mode has its own highscore and leaderboard.

Reach these scores in a single game to unlock a new bug and its weapon:

  • Easy, 300 points: Ant + ant killer spray
  • Easy, 500 points: Fly + electric swatter
  • Hard, 100 points: Beetle + spray gun
  • Hard, 300 points: Spider + pesticide sprayer

A congratulations message appears at the end of the game when you unlock something.

Go to User Settings → Game and click any unlocked bug or weapon. Locked ones are crossed out. Your bug also changes the game's background and the shoes you'll need to avoid.

The High Scores page shows the top 5 highscores for Easy and Hard mode from players who have played on this browser.

Go to User Settings → Game → Mute game audio and click the speaker button to turn game sounds on or off.

Your account, highscores, and unlocks are saved in this browser, so clearing your browser data or switching devices will reset them. Your chosen bug, weapon, and mute setting reset to the defaults each time you log in.
